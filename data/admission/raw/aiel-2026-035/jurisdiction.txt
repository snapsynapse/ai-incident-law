2025-1212. State v. Ford.
Summit C.P. No. CR-2013-04-1008-A. On appellant’s motion to strike the
December 31, 2025 notice of filing of the record, motion to complete the record
with omitted transcripts at state expense, and motion to order Summit County
Common Pleas clerk to complete the record. Motions granted. Appellant’s
expedited-ruling request regarding June 9, 2026 motions granted. The Summit
County court reporter is ordered to prepare, at appellee’s expense, the transcripts
from and the exhibits associated with the hearings on the following dates:
November 8, 2023, January 30, 2024, and August 6-7, 2024. The Summit County
Court of Common Pleas clerk shall file a complete copy of the record, including
the transcripts and exhibits from the omitted hearings, within 45 days or show
cause why she should not be held in contempt for failing to do so. Sua sponte,
briefing schedule stayed and will be reset after the Summit County Court of
Common Pleas clerk has transmitted the record.
