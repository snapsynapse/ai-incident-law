HISTORICAL SEARCH The District Clerks Office observes the recommendations from The Texas State Library Archive Commission for preservation of historical records. Our preservation efforts focus on records dating from 1837-1951. We are also in the process of digitizing these records to make them easily accessible online. Manage Client/Party Notifications Several County Criminal Courts at Law and District courts have reported intermittently slow system performance for Harris County District Clerk web site users. These slowdowns are causing longer than usual wait times for external users such as public users, law firms, and commercial vendors. We ask commercial customers who run data scraping scripts to help ease the system slowdown time by instead using our Public Datasets that provide a large quantity of information for download. If there are additional elements of data not currently within the datasets that you would like to see added, you may contact using the information in the “Contact Us” link at the bottom of the page.
